Switch through an overnight fund

Today a switch has one From and one To. Operations often go Equity → Overnight today, then Overnight → Equity a few days later, and raise those as two unrelated tasks. This demo shows one form that records both moves, files them as two linked tasks each with its own sub-tasks, and marks the first one Completed on its own, so admins can see it. The final switch falls on the 6th working day.

Demo only. Nothing here reads from or writes to the CRM. The client and funds are samples.

Step 3

How we would build it

Small, and built on what already exists. Nothing about a direct switch changes.

On the form

  • A Route choice on the Switch template: Direct (today) or Through an overnight fund.
  • A "Park in" field, filled with the same fund house's overnight fund, changeable to a liquid fund.
  • A "second switch after N working days" box, 6 by default.
  • The same-fund-house rule checked on all three schemes.

On the tasks

  • Submitting files switch 1 (From → Overnight) as a switch task marked "1 of 2", with three sub-tasks: the switch, informing the client, and checking it is done.
  • When switch 1 is marked Completed, switch 2 (Overnight → To, all units) is filed by itself, marked "2 of 2", on the 6th working day, linked to switch 1, with four sub-tasks: the switch, informing the client, checking it is done and updated in Wealth Elite, and Goal Mapping for the RM.
  • Switch 2 uses the "switch in progress, not yet reflected" pick added on 21 Sep, so it does not wait for InvestWell.

What admins see

  • Switch 1 appears in Today › Completed on the day it is done, as its own task.
  • Switch 2 appears on its due day and in Completed when done.
  • Each shows "1 of 2" / "2 of 2" and opens the other, so the whole move reads as one story.

Questions for Reshma before we build

  1. How many days between the two switches: answered, 6 working days.
  2. Always an overnight fund, or sometimes a liquid fund? The demo lets them change it.
  3. Switch 2 amount: always all units that landed in the overnight fund? The demo assumes yes.
  4. Who gets switch 2: the same Operations person as switch 1? The demo assumes yes.
  5. If switch 1 fails or is cancelled, switch 2 is never filed. Is that right?
  6. Sub-task days: the demo counts working days for every sub-task (check on the 3rd working day, Goal Mapping on the 6th after switch 2). Today's Switch template counts calendar days. Is working days right for this flow?
